Case studies

Four business problems, four dashboards

Each case starts with a question a manager would ask, then answers it with a dashboard and a recommendation. All data is generated for this portfolio.

Business problem

A first-time buyer in Hyderabad has a budget and no fair way to compare micro-markets.

Questions answered
  • Where is price per sq ft lowest for my budget?
  • Which areas pay the best rent?
  • Which listings are priced under their market?
Method

Cleaned 772 raw listings, modelled medians and yield with SQL window functions, and added a 24-month price index.

Sample data. The 772 raw listings here are generated for this demo around typical micro-market price ranges, with duplicates, missing values and typos added on purpose so the cleaning step is real. They are not live market quotes.
